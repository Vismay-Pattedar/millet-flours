The flours

Pick one to see how it tastes and what it's for. Every flour comes in 500 g and 1 kg packs.

    Raw flour tastes raw. Roasting the grain first gives the flour a nutty smell, takes away that raw edge, and helps it keep longer in the tin.

    Cleaned

    The grain is sieved, picked over by hand, washed and dried in the sun.

    Roasted

    Dry-roasted a few kilos at a time on a low flame, stirred until it turns a shade darker.

    Ground

    Milled once it has cooled, sieved, and packed the same day.
